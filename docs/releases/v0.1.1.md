# Emot-ID v0.1.1

Patch release aligning the Romanian product voice with private self-reflection and correcting the
Romanian appearance control discovered during the release audit.

## Changes

- Romanian user address is consistently informal singular across onboarding, Today, discovery,
  generated synthesis, Reflection, Journal, settings, privacy, support, and crisis guidance.
- A copy contract rejects polite-plural Romanian forms in both static UI and generated synthesis.
- Playwright and native acceptance adapters follow the new localized accessible names.
- The Romanian `Luminos` / `Întunecat` control has stable segment width and text padding at compact
  and desktop viewports.

English behavior, emotion models, safety gating, persistence, privacy, and external Google AI Mode
handoff semantics are unchanged.

## Verification

- Live Romanian review: onboarding, Today, guided arrival, Reflection, settings, privacy, and
  light/dark presentation.
- Lint, unit/integration, acceptance contract, bilingual and psychological-copy audits, TypeScript,
  production build, and performance budgets: 86 files / 685 tests, pass.
- Mobile Safari and Mobile Chrome Playwright matrix, including Romanian segment geometry at
  `320px` and `1280px`: 258/258, pass.
- Production PWA lifecycle and mobile performance probes: pass.

## Evidence Limits

The accepted external-evidence limits from `v0.1.0` are unchanged: no distinct low-tier Android,
six moderated participants, complete human TalkBack J1-J9 matrix, or working native SafariDriver
activation transport. These are evidence waivers, not known product defects.

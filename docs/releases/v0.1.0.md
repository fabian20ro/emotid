# Emot-ID v0.1.0

First formal release of the completed mobile-first Emot-ID experience.

## What Ships

- Direct affect placement, guided arrival, quick words, Body Compass, Word Ladder, and Plutchik
  discovery routes.
- Tentative Reflection with explicit fit, correction, rejection, optional needs, and a chosen next
  step.
- Local-only Journal for reflections and guided exercises, with export and deletion controls.
- Deterministic current-session support gating and actionable crisis resources.
- English and Romanian interfaces, light/dark themes, keyboard and screen-reader semantics,
  compact reflow, reduced motion, and offline PWA updates.
- Optional Google Search AI Mode link that sends only selected emotion names.

## Privacy

No account, backend, analytics, telemetry, or cloud sync. Reflections stay in browser storage on
the user's device unless the user explicitly opens an external link or exports local data.

## Accepted Limitations

- No distinct low-tier Android was available. Its hardware performance gate is deferred.
- Six moderated participant sessions were unavailable. Empirical comprehension validation is
  deferred; synthetic review is not treated as participant evidence.
- Human TalkBack passed six bounded browser/installed checkpoints, not the complete J1-J9 matrix.
- Safari 26.6 native automation is blocked by inert SafariDriver click transport before product
  journeys. WebKit, historical native Safari, and other browser evidence remain supporting only.
- Physical iPhone and VoiceOver evidence are outside this project's scope; iOS Simulator results do
  not claim physical screen-reader behavior.

These limitations are release-evidence waivers, not known product failures. No unresolved
release-blocking product defect was reproduced.

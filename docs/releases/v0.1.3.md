# Emot-ID v0.1.3

Corrective release for privacy integrity, core check-in accessibility, provenance, and persisted
data trust found by the August expert and measurement-backed audit.

## Changes

- Makes delete-all exclusive: pending local writes are aborted and physically drained before both
  stores and in-memory workflow state are cleared.
- Keeps Privacy switches aligned in compact English and Romanian layouts across light and dark
  themes.
- Adds neutral-origin Affect language, visible SVG focus, contrast-safe selected chips, and
  automatic completion reveal for Affect and Plutchik.
- Preserves the actual traversed Word Ladder path for multi-parent emotions and gives branch, leaf,
  completion, and removal actions explicit accessible names.
- Requires an explicit Yes or Partly fit before exposing the existing Google AI Mode handoff. Its
  `udm=50`, reviewed prompt, and emotion-name-only query contract are unchanged.
- Exposes qualitative body-match provenance, crisis save state, journal-exercise load failures,
  fail-fast persisted-record validation, and one crisis-resource URL source.
- Adds the complete product-policy gate to CI and removes bounded documentation, token, and dead
  bridge debt.
